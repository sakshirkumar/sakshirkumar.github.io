    • Per-field routing costs 1.78x less than one global threshold for the same 4,817 reviews

About

Online banking and digital payments, tested from the first scenario to the release gate.

Defects reach developers with reproduction steps, expected and actual results, severity and evidence. I hold a Master's in Quality Systems from Concordia University.
